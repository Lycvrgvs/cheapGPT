Merge: `{{cmd}} merge <id>[,<id>]` merges the builders' branches, each committed in its own worktree cut from BASE. The tool refuses paths both builders changed: the allocation was wrong, so re-allocate; never reconcile by hand. Judge the two results against the one-block: keep each part that does what the block names, and say in one line why. You may commit only the seam (wiring, imports, a shared signature the handoffs fixed); anything else goes back to its builder. Then run the affected path on the merged HEAD.
